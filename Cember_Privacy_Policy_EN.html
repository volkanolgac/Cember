1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ÇEMBER handles information when you use the mobile game. ÇEMBER is designed primarily to run game logic and store game progress locally on your device. We do not require an account, email address, telephone number, or other direct registration information to play the game.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ÇEMBER uses browser local storage within the application to save gameplay information and preferences. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Game progress and completed stages;
  • Tournament and adventure progress;
  • Scores, achievements, combinations, and similar gameplay statistics;
  • Selected game settings, teams, skins, and other preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not intended to be uploaded to a personal account or stored by the developer on a dedicated application database.

3. Online Multiplayer

ÇEMBER includes an optional online multiplayer mode. If you choose to use multiplayer features, the game communicates with third-party public MQTT services over secure WebSocket connections to allow two players to establish and synchronize a game session.

During an online multiplayer session, the game may transmit:

  • A temporary six-digit room code;
  • A temporary randomly generated client/session identifier;
  • The player name or profile information entered or selected within the game;
  • Selected team and ready-status information;
  • Game inputs, scores, player positions, ball and game-state information;
  • Connection timing and ping information required for synchronization.

This information is transmitted only to support the multiplayer session. The developer does not operate a separate user-account database for this information and does not intentionally use multiplayer messages to build personal profiles.

Third-party multiplayer infrastructure:
  • HiveMQ Public MQTT Broker
  • EMQX Public MQTT Broker

These services are third-party infrastructure. Their own privacy policies, logging practices, and terms may apply to network connections made to their services. Network-level metadata such as an IP address may be visible to the infrastructure provider as part of normal Internet communication.

4. Network and Technical Information

The application can detect whether the device is connected to a network and may use connection type information to manage the multiplayer experience. The application itself does not request or intentionally collect your precise physical location, contacts, photos, or address book.

Standard Internet communication can also expose technical information, such as an IP address, to the network or third-party service being contacted. Such information is handled by the relevant network or service provider according to its own policies.

5. Permissions

The Android application package may declare permissions required by the WebView application shell, including Internet/network access and vibration. The game does not use the microphone as a gameplay feature.

6. Advertising and Analytics

ÇEMBER does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising profiles and does not require third-party advertising identifiers for its core gameplay. No dedicated analytics or advertising SDK is intentionally used by the game to create user profiles.

7. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information. Information is transmitted to third-party multiplayer infrastructure only when necessary to provide the optional online multiplayer functionality.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on the device until it is removed by the user, application storage is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Multiplayer session messages are transient communications used for real-time gameplay. The developer does not maintain a permanent player account or gameplay database for ÇEMBER.

If you have a privacy-related request, you may contact the developer at volkanolgac@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

ÇEMBER does not knowingly request or require children to provide personal information such as an email address, telephone number, or real-world identity to play the game.

Because the optional multiplayer mode can involve a player-provided display name and network communication, parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online multiplayer features where appropriate.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact volkanolgac@gmail.com.

10. Security

The application uses secure WebSocket connections for its supported online multiplayer brokers and uses an isolated local WebView environment for its application assets. No security method can guarantee absolute protection against every possible threat, but reasonable technical measures are used to protect the application and its communications.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available with the application or at the location where this policy is published.
